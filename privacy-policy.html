Privacy Policy

Last updated:

1. Who we are

AzubaTrends ("we", "us", "our") operates this website to sell handmade and hand-loomed home goods, delivered within Kolkata and the rest of West Bengal, India. This policy explains what personal information we collect when you use this site, why we collect it, and how it's handled.

2. Information we collect

We collect only what's needed to process your order and support you afterwards:

  • Contact & delivery details: name, phone number, email address, delivery address, city, and pincode — collected at checkout.
  • Order details: items purchased, quantities, prices, payment method (UPI or Cash on Delivery), and — for UPI orders — the payment screenshot you upload for verification.
  • Order history: a record of past orders and their status (Pending, Shipped, Delivered, Cancelled, etc.), kept so we can help you with any issue relating to a past purchase.
  • Contact form messages: your name, email, subject, and message when you write to us via the Contact Us page.
  • Reviews: if you choose to leave a product review, we store the rating, comment, and any photo you attach. Reviews are shown publicly against the product but are not linked to your name — you're shown as an anonymous "Guest".
  • Browsing data: items in your Cart and Wishlist are stored locally in your browser (not on our servers) so they persist between visits on the same device.
  • Technical & analytics data: standard technical information (like IP address, browser type, and pages visited) collected automatically for security (like preventing spam/abuse on forms) and, where enabled, basic site-usage analytics.

We do not require you to create an account to shop with us — checkout is guest-only, and we don't ask for any information beyond what's listed above.

3. How we use your information

  • To process, deliver, and communicate with you about your order (including verifying UPI payments).
  • To respond to questions, complaints, or requests you send via the Contact Us form or email.
  • To process returns, exchanges, and refunds under our Refund & Cancellation Policy.
  • To send order-status updates (e.g. "Shipped", "Delivered") and, if you've opted in, browser push notifications about offers or restocks.
  • To prevent spam and abuse on our forms (contact form, reviews, stock-notification requests) using basic rate-limiting.
  • To improve the site, based on aggregate, non-identifying usage patterns.

We do not sell your personal information to anyone, and we do not use it for purposes beyond those listed here.

4. Third-party services we use

As a small online store, we rely on a few trusted third-party service providers to run this site. Each only receives the minimum information it needs to do its job:

  • Google Firebase (Firestore): securely stores order, product, and ticket data.
  • EmailJS: used to send order-confirmation and order-status emails, and to notify us when you submit the Contact Us form.
  • Telegram: used internally to alert the store owner instantly about new orders, reviews, and contact messages — this is for our own operational alerts and does not involve your data being made public.
  • ImgBB: used to host images uploaded with product reviews.
  • Website analytics (where enabled): aggregate, non-identifying traffic statistics to help us understand which pages are popular.

Each of these providers has its own privacy policy governing how it handles data on our behalf.

5. Cookies & local storage

We use your browser's local storage (not traditional tracking cookies) to remember your Cart and Wishlist between visits, and basic analytics identifiers if analytics is enabled. You can clear this at any time via your browser settings — doing so will simply empty your Cart/Wishlist on this device.

6. When we share information

We share your delivery details only with whoever is fulfilling and delivering your specific order (e.g. our delivery partner, where applicable). We do not share your information with advertisers or data brokers, and we don't sell it.

7. How we protect your information

Order and ticket data is stored in Firestore behind access rules that only allow the store owner (signed in as admin) to read customer details — nothing is publicly browsable. Forms on this site are also protected against spam/abuse using rate-limiting and hidden anti-bot fields.

8. Your choices

  • You can request a copy of, correction to, or deletion of your personal information by writing to us via the Contact Us page.
  • You can delete your own product review at any time using the delete option shown next to a review you've submitted (works only on the same device/browser you posted it from).
  • You can unsubscribe from browser push notifications at any time from your browser's notification settings.

9. Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time as our services change. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will always reflect the latest version.

11. Contact us

Questions about this Privacy Policy, or a request relating to your personal data, can be sent to azubatrends@gmail.com or via our Contact Us page.
