Shipping & Delivery Policy

Last updated:

1. Where we deliver

AzubaTrends currently delivers within West Bengal, India only — we're based in Kolkata, and our catalog is sourced largely from local artisans and sellers in and around the state. Addresses outside West Bengal cannot be accepted at checkout; the City field will only let you pick a serviceable location.

2. Dispatch & delivery timelines

Orders are typically dispatched within 1–3 business days of being placed (subject to stock availability), and delivered within a further few business days depending on your city/district within West Bengal. Exact delivery estimates for your pincode are shown at checkout using the "Check Delivery Date & Availability" tool on each product page.

3. Delivery partners

Some products ship directly through the specific delivery partner shown on that product's page (e.g. "This product comes from <Partner Name>"), since we work with multiple local artisans/sellers who each handle their own dispatch. Where no specific partner is shown, we handle dispatch ourselves.

4. Delivery & COD charges

Some products carry a small per-order delivery fee, shown clearly on the product page and at checkout before you pay — most orders have no separate delivery fee. Cash on Delivery (COD) orders may include an additional COD handling charge, also shown at checkout before you confirm the order.

5. Payment methods

We currently accept UPI (pay and upload your payment screenshot for quick verification) and Cash on Delivery. No card or net-banking gateway is integrated at this time.

6. Tracking your order

We don't yet offer live courier tracking links, but we do send order-status updates (Processing, Shipped, Delivered, etc.) to the email address you provide at checkout. For any update on a specific order, reach us via the Contact Us page with your order number.

7. Delivery issues

If your order hasn't arrived within the estimated window, or arrives damaged, please contact us within 7 days of the expected/actual delivery date — see our Refund & Cancellation Policy for what happens next.

8. Questions

For anything not covered above, reach us via the Contact Us page or email azubatrends@gmail.com.
