Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated:

1. Return & exchange window

We accept returns and exchanges within 7 days of delivery, for products marked as returnable (see "Non-returnable items" below — every product page clearly shows whether it's eligible, and for how many days).

2. Damaged, defective, or wrong item received

Contact us within 7 days of delivery via the Contact Us page (choose "Refund / Return" as the subject) with your order number and photos of the item. Once approved, we offer a free replacement or a full refund, and any return shipping cost in this case is on us.

3. Change of mind (non-defective returns)

For returnable products, we also accept returns if you simply don't want the item anymore, as long as it is unused, unwashed, and in its original packaging with tags intact. In this case, return shipping is paid by the customer, and the refund is issued once we receive and inspect the returned item.

4. Exchanges

Available for size/variant issues on the same product, subject to stock availability, within the same return window shown on that product's page.

5. Non-returnable / non-refundable items

Some products are marked non-returnable — this is always shown clearly on the product page itself, right below the product details, before you buy. This typically applies to items that can't reasonably be resold once opened or used, such as:

  • Customized, made-to-order, or personalized items
  • Items where hygiene is a factor (e.g. certain textiles/accessories once worn or used)
  • Items explicitly marked as a final-sale/clearance item

If a product page does not show a returns badge at all, please contact us before ordering to confirm its return eligibility.

Note: even non-returnable items remain covered under Section 2 above — if a non-returnable item arrives damaged, defective, or incorrect, you're still entitled to a replacement or refund.

6. How to start a return or exchange

  1. Go to the Contact Us page.
  2. Select "Refund / Return" as the subject.
  3. Include your order number, the item(s) you'd like to return, the reason, and (if damaged/defective) a photo.
  4. We'll confirm pickup/drop-off details and next steps by email or phone.

7. Refund method & timeline

Refunds are issued to the original payment method (or via UPI for Cash on Delivery orders) within 5–7 business days of the returned item passing inspection, or immediately for approved damaged/defective/wrong-item claims once confirmed.

8. Order cancellations

You can request cancellation any time before your order has been shipped by contacting us with your order number — we'll cancel it and issue a full refund (for prepaid/UPI orders) or simply mark it cancelled (for Cash on Delivery orders). Once an order has been shipped, it can no longer be cancelled, but you may still request a return once delivered, per the policy above.

9. About our invoices

AzubaTrends is a small reselling business and is not currently GST-registered. Orders are billed as a plain Bill of Supply (no GST is charged or shown on invoices) — this does not affect your right to a return, refund, or replacement under this policy.

10. Questions

For anything not covered above, reach us via the Contact Us page or email azubatrends@gmail.com.
