About Us

Our story

AzubaTrends is a small, Kolkata-based home goods store bringing hand-made and hand-loomed products from artisans and local sellers across West Bengal directly to your doorstep — without a showroom, and without the usual retail markup. "AzubaTrends" means courtyard: the shared, everyday space where a home actually happens.

What we sell

We curate and resell home goods sourced from independent artisans and small sellers in and around Kolkata — think terracotta, jute, brass, copper, and hand-stitched textiles. We don't manufacture these products ourselves; we work directly with the people who do, and pass their work on to you at a fair price.

Where we deliver

We currently deliver within Kolkata and the rest of West Bengal, India only. If an address falls outside that area, checkout will let you know before you pay — see our full Shipping & Delivery Policy.
